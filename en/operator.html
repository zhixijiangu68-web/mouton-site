About this site

I try things I'm curious about, make things, and keep a record of the process. I write about philosophy and how to live, and about the body and food, checking papers and data along the way.

It's a personal archive that records not only finished work, but things I'm still thinking about and things I'm still making. The site was first written in Japanese; this is the English edition.

How the articles are written

  • For articles on the body, food, and health, I check research papers and information from public bodies as far as I can, and cite sources in the text.
  • I try to keep separate what research has shown, what is not yet known, and my own impressions.
  • Health articles are general information and are not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a doctor.
  • If I find an error, I check it, correct it, and note the update date on the article.
  • English articles are translations of the Japanese originals. The numbers, sources, and claims are the same.
