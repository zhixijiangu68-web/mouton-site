Buying guides

What should you look at before you buy? These guides lay out what to check, based on the research and public information covered in the articles. The aim is to help you choose for yourself, rather than to push a particular brand.

How these guides are written

  • They're based on research and information from public bodies, with sources listed at the end of each article.
  • They never claim that something "works" or "cures." What's known and what isn't are kept separate.
  • Some products are sold mainly in Japan. The English edition carries no affiliate links.
