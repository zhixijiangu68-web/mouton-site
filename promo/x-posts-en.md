## T1 早く立ち直る技術から

### EN-1
```
You will fail. Get rejected. Make the wrong call. Lose money. Look stupid in front of people.

None of that is optional.

What is optional is how long you stay down.

Don't train to never fall. Train to get up faster.
```

### EN-2
```
Most people think resilience means being unbreakable.

It doesn't. It's the time between "that hurt" and "okay, what's next."

Shorten that gap and you'll outlast almost everyone.
```

### EN-3
```
Kids fall a hundred times a day and never call it failure. They call it playing.

Somewhere along the way, we started treating every fall as a verdict.

It was only ever practice.
```

### EN-4
```
Most of what I call fear turns out not to be danger.

It's discomfort, dressed up as danger.

Once I started telling the two apart, a lot of things I'd been avoiding became possible.
```

## T2 知性の5つのかたちから

### EN-5
```
Underrated forms of intelligence:

1. Noticing patterns before others do
2. Staying hopeful before there's a reason to
3. Saying less, judging better
4. Knowing when to care less
5. Never losing curiosity

None of these show up on a test.
```

### EN-6
```
The smartest people I know aren't the ones with the fastest answers.

They're the ones who stay curious long after everyone else has decided they already know.
```

### EN-7
```
Detachment isn't coldness.

It's choosing what deserves your energy and letting the rest pass by.

Caring less about most things is how you get to care deeply about a few.
```

### EN-8
```
Optimism that sounds a little delusional is often just someone seeing a future that hasn't arrived yet.

Realists predict. Optimists build.
```

### EN-9
```
Quiet people aren't always shy.

Some of them are just watching, listening, and deciding what's actually worth saying.
```

## T3 真実より、心地よい幻想から

### EN-10
```
Truth and comfort rarely come in the same package.

We say we want the truth. Usually we want a version of it we can live with.

The work is learning to want the first one anyway.
```

### EN-11
```
Nietzsche, in an 1865 letter to his sister:

"If you wish to strive for peace of soul and happiness, then believe; if you wish to be a disciple of truth, then inquire."

You don't get both for free.
```

### EN-12
```
A comfortable illusion feels like peace.

Until reality sends the bill.

Better to pay a little every day than all at once.
```

### EN-13
```
You'll never win a comparison you set up to lose.

You hold their best moment next to your worst one. Every time.

That's not a fair look at your life. It's an illusion that happens to hurt.
```

## T4 ソウルメイトより、自分の魂から

### EN-14
```
Everyone's looking for the right person.

Almost no one is looking for the right self.

Find the second one first. It changes who you'll recognize as the first.
```

### EN-15
```
Being alone isn't a waiting room for the next relationship.

It's the only time you get to hear your own voice without anyone else's in the mix.
```

### EN-16
```
It's hard to be found by the right people while you're still hiding from yourself.
```

## T5 目標の解像度を上げるから

### EN-17
```
"I want to be free" isn't a goal. It's a direction.

Free from what? Your schedule? A boss? A city? Certain people?

Each answer needs a completely different plan.
```

### EN-18
```
Vague goals feel inspiring and change nothing.

Write yours down in boring detail: what work, which people, how much money, how you stay healthy.

The details tell you what to learn next.
```

### EN-19
```
A goal without a system is a wish.

Decide what you'll do, when, and where.

The goal tells you where you're going. The system is what actually gets you there.
```

### EN-20
```
Don't ask, "What do I want my life to be?"

Ask, "What does a normal Tuesday look like in that life?"

The first question gives you a dream. The second one gives you a plan.
```
