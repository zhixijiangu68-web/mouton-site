# X の投稿文（英語・偉人の言葉・下書き100本）

英語のアカウント用。偉人の言葉を1行目か結びに置いて、手を止めさせる。

- **出典を確かめられる言葉だけを使う**。各投稿の下に出典を書いた。訳は英語で広く使われている訳か、それに近い形
- 有名なのに**本人の言葉ではないもの**は、「実は言っていない」という投稿（Q81〜Q95）にした。間違った出典で広めないため
- 短くする（多くは200字以内）。どれも URL なしで280字以内
- 健康・お金の話でも「効く」「必ず儲かる」とは書かない
- 投稿・予約はオーナーが行う

## Q1〜Q20 ストア派

### Q1
```
One of the richest men in Rome wrote:

"It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it."

Seneca had everything. He still worried about time.
```

### Q2
```
"Life is long, if you know how to use it."

Seneca, ~2,000 years before smartphones.
```
出典：Seneca, *De Brevitate Vitae* 2

### Q3
```
"We suffer more often in imagination than in reality."

Seneca wrote this in a letter to a friend.

Most of what you're dreading hasn't happened yet.
```
出典：Seneca, *Letters to Lucilius* 13

### Q4
```
"If a man knows not to which port he sails, no wind is favorable."

Seneca.

Busy isn't a direction.
```
出典：Seneca, *Letters to Lucilius* 71

### Q5
```
"Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life."

Seneca.

Not next Monday. Not next year.
```
出典：Seneca, *Letters to Lucilius* 101

### Q6
```
A formerly enslaved man taught this:

"Some things are within our power, while others are not."

Epictetus had no power over his body or his freedom.
He still found the one thing he controlled.
```
出典：Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 1

### Q7
```
"Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of things."

Epictetus.

2,000 years later, this line helped inspire modern cognitive therapy.
```
出典：Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 5／Still & Dryden (2012)（論理療法の源流としての位置づけ）

### Q8
```
"If you want to improve, be content to be thought foolish and stupid."

Epictetus.

The price of getting better is looking bad first.
```
出典：Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 13

### Q9
```
"No man is free who is not master of himself."

Epictetus.

He knew the difference. He'd been a slave.
```
出典：Epictetus, Fragments（Stobaeus 所収）

### Q10
```
The most powerful man in the world wrote in his private notebook:

"Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one."

Marcus Aurelius. Note to self.
```
出典：Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 10.16

### Q11
```
"The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way."

Marcus Aurelius.

The obstacle isn't blocking the path. It is the path.
```
出典：Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 5.20

### Q12
```
"Such as are your habitual thoughts, such also will be the character of your mind."

Marcus Aurelius.

Watch what you think about on autopilot.
```
出典：Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 5.16

### Q13
```
"How much more grievous are the consequences of anger than the causes of it."

Marcus Aurelius.

The thing that made you angry: small.
What you did because of it: not small.
```
出典：Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 11.18

### Q14
```
An emperor told himself:

"Do every act of your life as though it were the very last act of your life."

Not out of fear. Out of focus.
```
出典：Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 2.5

### Q15
```
Marcus Aurelius never meant for anyone to read Meditations.

It was a notebook. Written to himself, often on military campaigns.

The most read self-help book in history was a private diary.
```
出典：*Meditations*（原題 Ta eis heauton「自分自身へ」。出版を意図していなかったとされる）

### Q16
```
"Do not spoil what you have by desiring what you have not."

Epicurus.

You once wanted what you have now.
```
出典：Epicurus, *Vatican Sayings* 35

### Q17
```
Alexander the Great offered Diogenes anything he wanted.

Diogenes said:
"Stand out of my sunlight."

Some people can't be bought.
```
出典：Diogenes Laertius, *Lives of Eminent Philosophers* 6.38／Plutarch, *Life of Alexander* 14

### Q18
```
Seneca's test for a full life:

Not how long.
How you used it.

"Life is long, if you know how to use it."
```
出典：Seneca, *De Brevitate Vitae* 2

### Q19
```
Epictetus, on criticism:

If someone speaks badly of you, don't defend yourself.
Say: "He didn't know my other faults, or he'd have mentioned those too."
```
出典：Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 33.9（要約）

### Q20
```
The Stoics drew one line 2,000 years ago:

Up to you: your judgments, your actions.
Not up to you: everything else.

Most anxiety lives on the wrong side of that line.
```
出典：Epictetus, *Enchiridion* 1（要約）

## Q21〜Q35 実存・近代の哲学

### Q21
```
"He who has a why to live can bear almost any how."

Nietzsche wrote it.
Viktor Frankl quoted it after surviving Auschwitz.
```
出典：Nietzsche, *Twilight of the Idols*, Maxims 12／Frankl, *Man's Search for Meaning*

### Q22
```
"What does not kill me makes me stronger."

Nietzsche wrote this while suffering from illness for much of his life.

He wasn't speaking from comfort.
```
出典：Nietzsche, *Twilight of the Idols*, Maxims 8

### Q23
```
"God is dead" doesn't just mean "God doesn't exist."

Nietzsche was asking: when the thing that gave life meaning is gone, what do you live for?
```
出典：Nietzsche, *The Gay Science* 125

### Q24
```
"One must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star."

Nietzsche.

Your mess might be the material.
```
出典：Nietzsche, *Thus Spoke Zarathustra*, Prologue 5

### Q25
```
Nietzsche's test:

What if you had to live this exact life again, forever?
Every day. Every choice. Over and over.

Would that feel like a curse, or a gift?
```
出典：Nietzsche, *The Gay Science* 341（永劫回帰。要約）

### Q26
```
"Become who you are."

Nietzsche borrowed it from the Greek poet Pindar.

Not who they want. Not who you were.
```
出典：Nietzsche, *Ecce Homo* 副題「How One Becomes What One Is」／Pindar, Pythian 2

### Q27
```
A man pushes a boulder up a hill. It rolls down. Forever.

Camus: "One must imagine Sisyphus happy."

The pushing is the life.
```
出典：Camus, *The Myth of Sisyphus*

### Q28
```
"In the midst of winter, I found there was, within me, an invincible summer."

Albert Camus.
```
出典：Camus, "Return to Tipasa"（*Summer*）

### Q29
```
Sartre: "Man is condemned to be free."

Why "condemned"?

Because you can't not choose.
Doing nothing is a choice too.
```
出典：Sartre, *Existentialism Is a Humanism*

### Q30
```
"Existence precedes essence."

Sartre.

A knife is made for a purpose.
You aren't. You make yours.
```
出典：Sartre, *Existentialism Is a Humanism*

### Q31
```
"Hell is other people."

Sartre didn't mean people are bad.
He meant we get trapped by how others see us.
```
出典：Sartre, *No Exit*

### Q32
```
"Life can only be understood backwards; but it must be lived forwards."

Kierkegaard.

You won't see the pattern until later. Move anyway.
```
出典：Kierkegaard, Journals (1843)

### Q33
```
"Anxiety is the dizziness of freedom."

Kierkegaard.

You feel it most when you have the most choices.
```
出典：Kierkegaard, *The Concept of Anxiety*

### Q34
```
Written in the 1600s:

"The sole cause of man's unhappiness is that he does not know how to stay quietly in his room."

Pascal had never seen a phone.
```
出典：Pascal, *Pensées* 139（Krailsheimer 訳。よく出回る「人類の問題はすべて〜」は言い換え）

### Q35
```
"Life swings like a pendulum backward and forward between pain and boredom."

Schopenhauer.

Escape one, and you land in the other.
```
出典：Schopenhauer, *The World as Will and Representation*

```
Attributed to Heraclitus.

```

### Q40
```
"One swallow does not make a summer, nor does one day."

```

### Q41
```
"Life is short, the art long."

```

### Q42

```
Start where you're standing.
```

### Q43
```
"He who knows others is wise. He who knows himself is enlightened."

Lao Tzu.

Most of us study other people far more than ourselves.
```

### Q44

```
Rarer than it sounds.
```
出典：*Analects* 2.17

### Q45

```
The first line of Confucius' Analects:
```

### Q46

```
"If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles."

Sun Tzu.

```

### Q47

### Q48

```
The first verse of the Dhammapada:

"Mind is the forerunner of all things."
```
出典：*Dhammapada* 1

### Q49

```
One of Miyamoto Musashi's rules, written near the end of his life:

"Do nothing which is of no use."

By his own account, he never lost a duel.
```

## Q81〜Q95 実は言っていない

### Q81
```
Aristotle never said "We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit."

Will Durant wrote it in 1926, summarizing Aristotle.

Still good advice. Wrong name.
```
出典：Will Durant, *The Story of Philosophy* (1926)

### Q82
```
"Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results."

There's no record of Einstein ever saying it.

Ironically, we keep repeating the wrong attribution.
```
出典：Quote Investigator（アインシュタインの記録なし。1980年代の文献が初出とされる）

### Q83
```
Darwin never said "It is not the strongest species that survives, but the most adaptable."

It comes from a 1963 paraphrase by a management professor.

Even "survival of the fittest" was coined by Herbert Spencer.
```
出典：Leon Megginson (1963) の要約／Spencer, *Principles of Biology* (1864)

### Q84
```
Gandhi never said "Be the change you wish to see in the world."

What he wrote was:
"If we could change ourselves, the tendencies in the world would also change."

Less catchy. More honest.
```
出典：Gandhi, *Indian Opinion* (1913)

### Q85
```
"Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts."

There's no record of Churchill saying this.
```
出典：International Churchill Society（記録なしとしている）

### Q86
```
"Comparison is the thief of joy."

Usually credited to Theodore Roosevelt. There's no record of him saying it.

True either way.
```
出典：Quote Investigator（ルーズベルトの記録なし）

### Q87
```
"Be yourself; everyone else is already taken."

There's no record of Oscar Wilde writing it.

What he did write: "To love oneself is the beginning of a lifelong romance."
```
出典：Quote Investigator／Wilde, *An Ideal Husband*

### Q88
```
"Be kind, for everyone you meet is fighting a hard battle."

Not Plato. It's traced to a Scottish minister writing around the 1890s.
```
出典：Quote Investigator（Ian Maclaren〈John Watson〉）

### Q89
```
Henry Ford never said "If I had asked people what they wanted, they would have said faster horses."

There's no evidence he did.
```
出典：Quote Investigator／Harvard Business Review（Ford の記録なし）

### Q90
```
Einstein never called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world."

There's no record of it.

Compound interest is still worth understanding.
```
出典：Quote Investigator（アインシュタインの記録なし）

### Q91
```
"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."

Not found in Leonardo da Vinci's writings.
```
出典：Quote Investigator（レオナルドの記録なし）

### Q92

```
"Between stimulus and response there is a space. In that space is our power to choose our response."

Not found in Viktor Frankl's books.

What Frankl wrote: everything can be taken from a man but one thing, the freedom to choose one's attitude.
```
